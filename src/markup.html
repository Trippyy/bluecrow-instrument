Interaction & transition notes

Chapter change

Vertical scroll-snap, one product per screen. The environment plate settles from a 1.06 scale while the product rises and fades in behind it — depth arrives before detail, so each chapter reads as a space you enter rather than a slide that swaps.

Depth

Every product is a true alpha cutout composited over a defocused plate from its own shoot. The headline sits between those two layers, so the name passes behind the object. Nothing is masked and nothing is doubled.

Navigator

A fixed right rail carries all three numbers and abbreviations at once. The active cell turns blue; tapping any cell snaps to that chapter. Position in the sequence is always legible without a progress bar.

Route to purchase

“View product” lifts the product page up over the chapter. The chapter stays mounted underneath, so dismissing returns you to the exact point in the sequence rather than to the top.

Restraint

Blue appears three times and no more: the active navigator cell, the route label, and the purchase border. Everything else is white, grey, or a hairline.

Lines

One hairline weight runs through the whole system — the top bar, the rail cells, the specification rows and the purchase block. It is the only divider used anywhere.

Desktop

The same components re-anchor rather than re-flow: the product moves off-centre to the right, the headline bleeds further left behind it, and the information column settles bottom-left. The rail and the hairline weight are unchanged.

Reduced motion

Entrance transforms and smooth scrolling are both dropped when the viewer asks for reduced motion. The composition is identical at rest.
